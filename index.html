Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AccessComm completely free?

Yes! All lessons, quizzes, and certificates are 100% free with no registration required.

Do I need any prior knowledge to start?

No prerequisites at all. Our lessons are designed for complete beginners and progress at your pace.

Can I get a certificate after the quiz?

Absolutely. Complete the quiz and enter your name to generate and download a PDF certificate instantly.

What is the difference between ASL and other sign languages?

ASL (American Sign Language) is specific to North America. Different countries have their own sign languages, though we teach the ASL alphabet as a widely recognised starting point.
